Privacy Policy

Stupid Simple Tipping Calculator · Last updated: July 28, 2026

Stupid Simple Tipping Calculator does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

Information We Collect

None. The app does not request, collect, or transmit personal data. It contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising libraries, and no crash-reporting services.

How the App Works

Everything you enter — check amounts, restaurant type, service rating, and settings such as Smart Cap and Dark Mode — is processed entirely on your device. This information is never sent anywhere, and is not retained after you close the app.

Permissions

The app requests no sensitive device permissions. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, files, or device identifiers.

Sending Feedback

The app includes an optional button that opens your device's default email application with a message addressed to us. This is entirely voluntary, and nothing is sent unless you choose to write and send the email yourself. If you do contact us, we will see the email address you sent from along with whatever you include in your message. We use that only to read and respond to your feedback, and we do not add you to any mailing list or share it with anyone.

Third Parties

The app does not integrate any third-party services, and no data is shared with or sold to any third party.

Children's Privacy

Because the app collects no information from anyone, it collects no information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with an updated date above. Material changes will also be reflected in the app's Google Play listing.

Contact
